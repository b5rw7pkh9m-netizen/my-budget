How allocation works

1. This week’s share of gas and groceries.
2. Bills are paid off completely, earliest due date first (recurring, phone, insurance, then rent).
3. Whatever is left is split evenly between savings, groceries, gas and personal, until each reaches its monthly target.
4. Once every target is full, extra goes to savings.

This records the plan only; it does not move money or pay bills.
